# Code of conduct

Ionomos follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- **We pledge** to make taking part in this project a harassment-free
  experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability,
  ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
  experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance,
  race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- **Expected:** empathy and kindness; respect for differing opinions and
  experiences; giving and accepting constructive feedback; owning mistakes
  and learning from them; putting the community's good before our own.
- **Not acceptable:** sexualised language or imagery; trolling, insulting or
  derogatory comments, personal or political attacks; public or private
  harassment; publishing others' private information (an e-mail address, a
  lab's unpublished data) without their explicit permission; other conduct
  that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.
- **Scope:** all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
  public spaces where someone represents the project.
- **Enforcement:** report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer,
  @nichfoster, through the contact on the GitHub profile; not in a public
  issue. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The
  maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions and may warn,
  suspend or ban a participant, following the Covenant's enforcement
  guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

The full text is at the link above; it is the one that applies.
