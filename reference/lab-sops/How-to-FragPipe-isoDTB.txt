isoDTB via Fragpipe:
Works for standard isoDTB experiments that use iodoacetamide alkyne (C6) and (commercial) isoDTB reagents according to Zanon et al. The workflow is designed for Trypsin digested samples.
Can be adjusted for other reagents/enzymes!
