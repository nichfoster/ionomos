Only in one condition

Measured in at least 75% (and at least two) of one group's samples and in none of the other: often the strongest biology, and invisible to a t-test without imputation. Missing can also mean below detection, so confirm them.

Enrichment

Dose-response

A curve per feature across the doses (CurveCurator's 4-parameter log-logistic fit, ratio to the control). Only curves classed up or down have a potency worth reading; click a row or a point to draw its curve over the measured values.

Time course

Which features change over time (a moderated F-test across the time points), in which direction, and with which shape. Click a pattern to list its features; click a row to draw the feature over its replicates.

Liganded sites

Which cysteines each compound engages: a site is liganded when its competition ratio R reaches the threshold in enough replicates. This is the chemoproteomics convention, not a p-value; the volcano above tests whether a ratio differs from 1. Click a row to see the site's replicates.

Quality control

Methods

Quantities from DIA-NN (diann-output/report.pg_matrix.tsv) were read by Ionomos. Protein intensities were log2-transformed, contaminants removed, features kept when quantified in at least 50% of the samples of at least one condition, samples median-centred, missing values imputed from a normal distribution down-shifted by 1.8 SD with 0.3× the SD of each sample (Perseus-type). Differential abundance was tested with limma (linear model ~0 + condition, empirical-Bayes moderated t-statistics, 95% confidence intervals), following FragPipe-Analyst's test_limma. P-values were adjusted with the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure; features were called significant at |log2FC| ≥ 1 and adjusted p ≤ 0.05 (limma moderated t-test). Features measured in at least 75% (and at least two) of the samples of one group and in none of the other were listed separately as present/absent; their t-test p-values, where any, rest on imputed values. Over-representation of up- and down-regulated genes in gene sets (GO Biological Process, Hallmark, Reactome; Enrichr libraries) was tested with a one-sided hypergeometric test against all quantified genes, BH-adjusted. Coordinated shifts of whole gene sets were tested on every quantified gene, ranked by the moderated t-statistic, with a Wilcoxon rank-sum test whose variance is inflated by the set's inter-gene correlation (the approach of limma's camera), BH-adjusted. Sample quality was scored by identifications, correlation with replicates, spread around the group mean and leave-one-out CVs (robust z-scores); principal components were related to condition and replicate number (one-way ANOVA R²), and the share of unchanged features estimated from the p-value distribution (Storey's π0). Processing and statistics port FragPipeAnalystR / FragPipe-Analyst (Hsiao et al., J. Proteome Res. 2024, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.4c00294) and limma (Ritchie et al., Nucleic Acids Res. 2015).
