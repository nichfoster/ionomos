Methods

Raw files were searched with FragPipe using the lab's pinned DIA workflow, run automatically by Ionomos. Protein intensities were log2-transformed, contaminants removed, features kept when quantified in at least 50% of the samples of at least one condition, samples median-centred, missing values imputed from a normal distribution down-shifted by 1.8 SD with 0.3× the SD of each sample (Perseus-type). Differential abundance was tested with limma (linear model ~0 + condition, empirical-Bayes moderated t-statistics, 95% confidence intervals), following FragPipe-Analyst's test_limma. P-values were adjusted with the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure; features were called significant at |log2FC| ≥ 1 and adjusted p ≤ 0.05 (limma moderated t-test). Over-representation of up- and down-regulated genes in gene sets (GO Biological Process, Hallmark, Reactome; Enrichr libraries) was tested with a one-sided hypergeometric test against all quantified genes, BH-adjusted. Processing and statistics port FragPipeAnalystR / FragPipe-Analyst (Hsiao et al., J. Proteome Res. 2024, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.4c00294) and limma (Ritchie et al., Nucleic Acids Res. 2015).
