How far to trust this

  • Samples per group: DMSO 3, Drug 3.
  • Replicate agreement: median correlation of a sample with its own replicates r = 0.905 (lowest 0.822); median CV 21% (DMSO), 16% (Drug).
  • Missing values: 3.5% of the values were not measured. 3.5% of the values in the statistics are imputed (perseus).
  • Drug vs DMSO: 90 features tested, 5 up and 2 down. Hits resting on imputed values (half or more of a group imputed): 1 of 7.
  • Power: with 3 samples per group and this experiment's median spread (SD 0.28 log2), a change of |log2FC| ≥ 0.76 is found 80% of the time at p 0.05 (≥ 1.54 at p 0.001, closer to what survives the correction for many tests).
Each statement repeats a check of this analysis with its number. There is no overall score. Accuracy against a reference or known ratios: ionomos compare, ionomos benchmark.

A competition experiment: what the compound enriches against the control (across), and how much of that the competitor takes off (up). Specific targets are both, at the cut-offs above: the marked quadrant. Enriched but not competed points at unspecific binding. Click a point or a row.

Only in one condition

Measured in at least 75% (and at least two) of one group's samples and in none of the other: often the strongest biology, and invisible to a t-test without imputation. Missing can also mean below detection, so confirm them.

Quality control

Methods

Quantities from DIA-NN (diann-output/report.pg_matrix.tsv) were read by Ionomos. Protein intensities were log2-transformed, contaminants removed, features kept when quantified in at least 50% of the samples of at least one condition, samples normalised on feature ratios (each sample shifted by the median, over the 55 features with the most stable profile among those measured in every sample, of the feature's value minus its mean across samples), because median centring would have shifted the conditions against each other by 0.39 log2, missing values imputed from a normal distribution down-shifted by 1.8 SD with 0.3× the SD of each sample (Perseus-type). Differential abundance was tested with limma (linear model ~0 + condition, empirical-Bayes moderated t-statistics, 95% confidence intervals), following FragPipe-Analyst's test_limma. Samples in each comparison: Drug vs DMSO, 3 against 3. P-values were adjusted with the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure; features were called significant at |log2FC| ≥ 1 and adjusted p ≤ 0.05 (limma moderated t-test). Features measured in at least 75% (and at least two) of the samples of one group and in none of the other were listed separately as present/absent; their t-test p-values, where any, rest on imputed values. Over-representation of up- and down-regulated genes in gene sets (GO Biological Process, Hallmark, Reactome; Enrichr libraries) was tested with a one-sided hypergeometric test against all quantified genes, BH-adjusted. Coordinated shifts of whole gene sets were tested on every quantified gene, ranked by the moderated t-statistic, with a Wilcoxon rank-sum test whose variance is inflated by the set's inter-gene correlation (the approach of limma's camera), BH-adjusted. Sample quality was scored by identifications, correlation with replicates, spread around the group mean and leave-one-out CVs (robust z-scores); principal components were related to condition and replicate number (one-way ANOVA R²), and the share of unchanged features estimated from the p-value distribution (Storey's π0). Processing and statistics port FragPipeAnalystR / FragPipe-Analyst (Hsiao et al., J. Proteome Res. 2024, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.4c00294) and limma (Ritchie et al., Nucleic Acids Res. 2015).
